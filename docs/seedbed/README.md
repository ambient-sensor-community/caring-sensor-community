# CSC Seedbed

Welcome to the seedbed—the place for early-stage ideas. Here, we preserve seeds, research threads, proposals, assumptions, and parking-lot ideas.

## What Goes Here

- **Seed:** An early idea worth preserving.
- **Research Thread:** A question that may deserve investigation.
- **Proposal:** A possible course of action not yet accepted.
- **Assumption:** An important belief that should eventually be tested.
- **Parking-Lot Item:** Valuable but not timely right now.

## Important Notes

- Capturing an idea here doesn’t mean it’s a commitment.
- Accepted decisions move to the Decision Log.
- To add a seedbed entry, use our template and submit a pull request.

This is the garden where ideas can sprout later—no pressure, just potential!

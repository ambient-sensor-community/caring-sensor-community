# Caring Sensor Community Decision Log

## Version 1.0

### Format
Each decision includes a brief description, the date it was agreed upon, and any relevant context or rationale.

### Decisions

1. **Local Control of Data**
   - Description: Local control of household data is a high-priority principle for CSC.
   - Date: 6/6/2026 
   - Context: As we build the CSC network, we believe the resident, the parent, and the family on their data. No one else.


2. **Cathedral and Bazaar**
   - Description: The CSC will follow a "Cathedral and Bazaar" philosophy, encouraging open collaboration and healthy competition.
   - Date: 6/6/2026 
   - Context: "The Cathedral and The Bazaar", an essay by Eric Raymond about creating good open source software. We agree with this approach, using an open source community to create something. 
     
3. **Technical Certification**
   - Description: A technical certification program will be developed for community members.
   - Date: 6/6/2026 
   - Context: Even in the very early days of CSC, it is not difficult to see the benefit of a certification program for creating, deploying, using and, maintaining the CSC system. Customers and Community members of CSC can use certification to determine who is qualified to give them expert help.

4. **Certified Partner Status**
   - Description: Certified partner status will be a distinct category, separate from individual certifications.
   - Date: 6/6/2026 
   - Context: Many products, hardware and software, already exist in the marketplace related to AI and sensor networks. We believe that other products, whether proprietary or open source, can inter-operate with the CSC network. Our Certified Partner Status will provide a mechanism to identify, vet, and promote qualified partners. 

5. **CSC Overview Infographic, Version 0.3 Promoted to Github**
   - Description: Plain-language, non-technical explanation of the Caring Sensor Community
   - Date: 6/8/2026 
   - Context: expresses several settled CSC ideas cleanly: local control of data, a stable core platform, open collaboration, and technology that supports human relationships rather than replacing them. Those points align with the accepted Decision Log and the current CSC overview source.
